# Alder Mechanical Services Group

Fictional sample company; all figures are synthetic. Calendar financial year; USD. Commercial HVAC maintenance, emergency repair and parts across Midwest branches. 100 Alder Service, 200 Shared Services, 300 Summit (acquired Jan 2025), ELIM consolidation. Reporting uses debit-positive TB and positive revenue in P&L.

Monthly exports include balanced double-entry journals, January prior-year earnings closing, support schedules and an explicit intercompany elimination entity. The archive is a constructed history of a finance process, not a claim that these files existed in 2023.

Blue numbers: inputs. Teal formulas: calculations. All reporting facts are reconciled to monthly GL. Acquisition is an asset purchase: no parent investment account. Straight-line 84-month pooled fleet depreciation. Monthly tax provision 25%; quarterly cash payments. Cost-plus shared services at 4%. Annual insurance premiums amortized monthly.
